The proposal:

Project Goal

Redesign the Hack VC website so founders and LPs know in seconds who you back, trust the firm, and know how to reach you.

Keep what already works: the simple navigation, the thesis-driven positioning and the hacker character. Then make it read as institutional, credible and clear to every LP and founder who lands on it.

Designed and built in Webflow in a two-week sprint, with the LP Data Room connected to Attio.

Keep & Change

Keep

  • The simple, concise navigation
  • The thesis-driven positioning and Insights
  • The logo and icon
  • The terminal character, made more institutional

Change

  • Key figures on the first screen
  • A portfolio of only active, strong companies
  • Plainer copy on who you back
  • A lighter look, with a light mode
  • Type that reads well at small sizes
  • A clearly ordered team page

Our Process

  1. 1. Creative Direction & Moodboards

    We start by settling the open questions in the brief: color (dark with a light mode, green-tinted or light first), typeface (grotesk, serif or geometric, each with mono accents) and navigation.

    We present a moodboard for each direction, with 1kx, Electric Capital and Dragonfly as reference points, so the team can choose with confidence before we commit to the full site.

  2. 2. Hero Design + Landing Page Wireframe

    Once the direction is approved, we design the hero in full detail: the “We Back Hackers.” headline, the key figures and the four focus areas, all on the first screen.

    At the same time, we wireframe the rest of the landing page. This gives us alignment on both:

    • How the site should look
    • How the full experience should flow

  3. 3. Full Design & Webflow Build

    Once the hero and structure are approved, we design the full landing page and move straight into Webflow. This includes:

    • Desktop and mobile design
    • Motion and interactions
    • Webflow development
    • Attio integration for LP Data Room requests
    • SEO and AI search setup
    • Final QA and launch
