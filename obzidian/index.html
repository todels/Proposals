ObzidianLaunch Video Proposal

Presented by Polify

The proposal:

Project Overview

Create a 1:45 launch video that introduces Obzidian in a high-impact, premium way.

One film that shows the trading floor building itself around the trader: real product, no mockups, with the founder on camera from first frame to last.

Built from the approved launch script, finished in motion, sound and color, and delivered as a master plus social-ready cuts.

The Film

Nine scenes, as written in the launch script.

0:00–0:10
The hook. Panes fly in and arrange themselves around the founder.
0:10–0:18
The reveal. Panes snap into one floor. Obzidian wordmark.
0:18–0:33
The problem. Twelve tabs, three explorers, a chat that never sleeps.
0:33–0:40
The tension. Everything freezes and fades to black.
0:40–0:58
The floor builds itself. A prompt, and the panes rebuild live.
0:58–1:10
Yours. Drag, swap and ask for panes. One workspace per strategy.
1:10–1:24
Your rules. A rule in plain words, then the trigger notification.
1:24–1:34
Always on. The laptop closes. Automations keep running across chains.
1:34–1:45
The line. Some trade on vibes. We profile. CTA and wordmark.

The Flow

The film moves in four parts. The hook earns attention, the problem and tension make the viewer feel the pain, the solution shows the floor working, and the outro lands the line and the CTA.

Hook. Panes fly in around the founder, then snap into one floor. Obzidian wordmark and the founder’s name.

1:34–1:45

On length: the script has about 170 spoken words, roughly 70 seconds of voiceover spread across 1:45. That leaves long stretches with no voice. We suggest either tightening the film to around 60–75 seconds, or keeping 1:45 and filling those stretches with deliberate product moments and sound design so the pace never drops.

Scope of Work

  1. 1. Creative Direction & Visual References

    Establish the visual direction, pacing, references, and overall tone for the launch video.

  2. 2. Storyboard / Scene Planning

    Map out all nine scenes of the 1:45 sequence before animation begins.

  3. 3. Motion Design & Animation

    Produce the full animated video based on the approved storyboard and script, including the pane system and product UI sequences.

  4. 4. Sound Design & Music Integration

    Add music, sound effects, and audio treatment to support the pacing and overall impact.

  5. 5. Final Color & Polish

    Refine timing, transitions, color, effects, and overall finish.

  6. 6. Final Delivery

    Delivery of one 1:45 master launch video in the agreed format.

  7. 7. Revisions

    Reasonable revisions throughout production to ensure the final result is aligned with Obzidian’s expectations.
