Landing Page Proposal

Presented by Polify

The current site shows work but does not feel like a studio. The hero opens on a gradient, one line of copy and three stat counters. Counters like these are a lead-gen agency pattern, not something top studios put in a hero. They make the operation look smaller and cheaper than it is. Stats belong in case studies, where they have context.

The rest of the page has the same issue. Work sits in tiles with nothing framing it. There is no sense of a place, a team or a process behind it, so Shown Media reads as more interchangeable than it actually is.

What the site needs to communicate:

  • Quality. The production value of the work is high. The site should match it in craft, pacing and polish, not undercut it with a generic layout.
  • A real studio. Shown Media is not a freelancer with a Framer template. It is a proper operation with offices in New York and Sarajevo and a large team behind every campaign. The site should make that scale felt, not just stated.
  • Scale of impact. The results are real, but they should come through the presentation of the work itself and through case studies, not through counters in the hero.

So we created a concept:

Our focus was to make the work feel big, and make the studio feel real.

The new hero is a space. Three curved screens wrap around the viewer, playing Shown Media work at full scale. Two small people (representing team members) stand in front, which sets the scale and puts people behind the work. The headline sits bottom left, the positioning line bottom right, so the visual lands first and the copy second.

The proposal:

Project Goal

Redesign and develop the Shown Media website into a significantly more premium, high-impact experience.

The goal is to retain the existing blue brand identity while creating something that feels sexy, elevated and visually memorable for the tech, AI and VC audience.

We want the reaction to be simple:

“Holy shit, this is sexy.”

Our Process

1. Creative Direction & Moodboards

We begin by exploring multiple visual directions through moodboards.

This allows us to align on the right style, references, typography, colors, motion and overall feeling before committing to the full website.

The goal is to get as close as possible to the vision Shown wants to achieve from the very beginning.

2. Hero Design + Landing Page Wireframe

Once the direction is approved, we design the hero section in full detail to establish the final visual language.

At the same time, we create the wireframe and content structure for the rest of the landing page.

This gives us alignment on both:

  • How the site should look
  • How the full experience should flow

3. Full Website Design & Development

Once the hero and structure are approved, we design the complete landing page and move directly into development.

This includes:

  • Full desktop design
  • Mobile / responsive design
  • Motion and interactions
  • Development
  • Content implementation
  • Responsive optimization
  • Final QA and polish
  • Launch-ready delivery
